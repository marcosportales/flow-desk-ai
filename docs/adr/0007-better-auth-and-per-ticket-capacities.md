# Better Auth sessions; Requester and Operator are per-Ticket capacities, not roles

Everyone who signs in is a User of the same company, authenticated with Better Auth (email and password, cookie sessions, seeded fictional Users). A User has no role column, only zero or more Team memberships: they are Requester of the Tickets they created and Operator of the Tickets whose Team they belong to, and can be both on the same Ticket. We rejected a global `requester`/`operator` role per User because it models two kinds of person that do not exist in a company (an IT Operator who files a ticket about their own laptop is a Requester), and it would force separate accounts to demonstrate both views. The demo login offers "Sign in as…" shortcuts, switched off by an environment variable.
